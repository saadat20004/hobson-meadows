Life at the meadow

Slow mornings by the pond. Fireside nights under the stars.

Good to know before you go

  • Yurts have no electricityLanterns are provided for a warm glow
  • Pack layersDays run warm, nights get cool, and there is no heat
  • PetsFree on RV & tent sites. Blackberry is the only pet friendly yurt ($55 per pet, per night)
  • Drinking waterBring your own for most camp sites. Pondside Paradise & Wildernest #5 have potable water
  • No hookupsRV & tent sites are dry camping (the event venue has power & water)
  • Easy accessDrive right up to your site, close to I-5. Exact address shared after booking
